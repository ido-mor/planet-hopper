# Handoff — current session

Scope: replace the intro sequence with pixel-art sprites, and propagate that art
to the gameplay screens. Audio, the two-tap unlock, and the 3-2-1 pacing against
`sounds/3 2 1 go_*.mp3` are unchanged — this was a visual pass only.

## What changed

- **New sprites** in `assets/sprites/`, generated by `tools/make-sprites.py`
  (rocket idle/fire/small, countdown digits, gantry, life pips) and
  `tools/make-astronaut-sheet.py` (12-frame walk cycle converted from the
  owner's `walking_2.gif`, transparent background, hand-seeded palette).
- **Intro rebuilt**: one `#introStage` holding gantry + astronaut + rocket,
  replacing the old `#introScene` / `#blastOffScene` pair. Doors, ladder, the
  CSS-box rockets, the green ground strip and the launch scaffold are gone.
  Added a static starfield.
- **Title** now scatters letter-by-letter (`buildTitleLetters()` +
  `titleLetterFly`) instead of fading. Font and colour cycling untouched.
- **Boarding** is a side entry: the astronaut walks up the gantry and is
  occluded by the hull, then fades — so nothing is left standing on the ramp
  when the rocket lifts off.
- **`GO!`** is a new sprite in the same style as the supplied 3/2/1, and it is
  the ignition beat: the flame lights and the rocket climbs on that step.
- **Gameplay art** swapped to the same sprites: map ship, life pips,
  level-complete rocket, dancing astronaut.
- `sw.js` `CACHE_NAME` bumped to `planet-hopper-v10` with the sprites added to
  `REQUIRED_ASSETS`.

## Verified

Landscape 844x390 in a browser: two-tap unlock, title scatter, walk up the ramp,
hull occlusion, 3 / 2 / 1 / GO!, ignition and liftoff with the gantry standing,
hand-off into gameplay, Play Again replay resetting cleanly, map ship and life
pips at all three life states, level-complete overlay, portrait rotate guard.

## Known, not addressed

- On the level-complete overlay the rocket and astronaut float above the planet
  band rather than standing on it. This is pre-existing (`.level-complete-surface`
  has `padding-bottom: 20%` while the planet disk is positioned separately) and
  was not introduced by the sprite swap — but it is more noticeable now.
- Not yet play-tested on a real iPhone / Home Screen PWA.
